# Committee guide

[Back to README](../README.md)

How to run the choir through the site, task by task. Everything here is done from the Committee section of the members' area. On a computer the committee pages are in the sidebar; on a phone, tap the Committee button at the end of the navigation strip. [User journeys](USER_JOURNEYS.md) has screenshots of most of these tasks.

## Start of a new term

1. Open Choir Settings.
2. Change the current term (for example "Spring 2027"), and check the subscription amount, deadline and bank details.
3. Save.

Changing the term starts a new subscription period: everyone shows as Unpaid in Financials, and singers see the subscription card on their dashboard. Payments for earlier terms are kept and can still be viewed.

## A new singer joins

1. Open Members.
2. Under Invite a Member, enter their email address. Their name and voice part are optional but save them a step.
3. Send the invitation.

They get an email with a link to set a password, valid for three days. Until they accept, they are listed under Pending Invitations, where Resend sends a fresh link. Once they sign up they automatically get an RSVP for every future event.

## A singer leaves

1. Open Members and click their name.
2. Untick Active and save.

They can no longer log in, and they drop out of RSVPs, the ledger and broadcasts. Their attendance history is kept. Tick Active again to bring them back. Accounts cannot be deleted from the site, so nothing is lost by mistake.

## Changing someone's role

Open Members, click their name, and change the ticks:

| Tick | Means |
| --- | --- |
| Singer | Gets RSVPs, appears in the ledger, polls, rosters and broadcasts. Untick for someone who has a login but does not sing. |
| Under 18 | Flagged on the Duty of Care roster and the members list |
| Exempt from subs | Shown as Exempt in the ledger and never asked to pay |
| Committee | Can use every committee page |
| Active | Can log in |

You cannot remove your own committee access or deactivate yourself, so the committee can never lock itself out by accident. Ask another committee member.

## Setting up a project

1. Open Projects and create the project, with its dates.
2. Set its status. Planning projects can have availability polls. Active projects appear on members' dashboards. Archived projects are history.
3. Choose its repertoire. To add a piece that is not in the library yet, use "Add a new piece to this project", which takes you to the Music Library with the project already chosen.
4. Optionally write a public summary. It appears on the Events page when one of the project's performances is next.

## Scheduling rehearsals

1. Open Schedule.
2. Fill in the form below the list: project, type, date and time, place.
3. Save. Every singer gets an RSVP for it straight away.

To add a rehearsal plan, click Edit on the event. The plan appears under the date on singers' dashboards, so it is a good place for "bars 1 to 24 of the Byrd, then the full piece".

Deleting an event also deletes its RSVPs, and asks first. Events in archived projects cannot be deleted.

## Putting a performance on the public Events page

1. Open Schedule and click Edit on the performance.
2. Under Public Listing, tick "Show on the public Events page".
3. Give it a public title and a short description. Add a ticket link and an end time if there are any.
4. Save.

The performance appears on the Events page as the next event until it has happened, then moves to Recent Performances and later to the archive. If the time is not fixed yet, set the start time to 00:00 and the page shows "time TBC". Rehearsals never appear publicly.

## Checking who is coming

Open RSVPs. Event Logistics shows each upcoming event's replies by status. You can change anyone's reply, for example when someone tells you in person. "Email Reminder" opens your own email program with everyone who has not replied in the BCC line.

Member Overview shows each singer's attendance over time.

## Adding scores and practice tracks

1. Open Music Library. If the piece is new, add it with its composer and title.
2. Click the piece.
3. Choose the voice part (or Full Choir for scores everyone uses).
4. Choose one or more files: PDF, MP3, M4A or WAV, up to 50 MB each. Each file becomes its own item, named after the file. Alternatively, paste a link to a recording elsewhere, such as YouTube, and give it a name.
5. Upload.

The files appear straight away on that voice part's hub. Very large WAV files can time out while uploading; convert them to MP3 first. Deleting an item deletes the file too.

## Asking about availability

1. Open Polling.
2. Choose a planning project, write the question, add the proposed date and any notes.
3. Create the poll. Members see it on Looking Ahead.

Results show the counts, each member's note, and how many have not answered, with a link that opens a reminder email to them. Close the poll once you have decided; it disappears from Looking Ahead. You can reopen it.

## Subscriptions

Open Financials. It shows the current term; use the term selector to look at earlier ones.

- When someone pays, check the amount and method and click Log. Their dashboard card disappears.
- Edit or Delete corrects a mistake.
- Mark Exempt for anyone who should not pay. Un-exempt reverses it.

Logging a payment while viewing an earlier term records it against that term.

## Emailing the choir

1. Open Broadcast and start a new broadcast.
2. Choose who it goes to: everyone, or one voice part.
3. Write the subject and message, and save the draft.
4. The preview shows exactly how the email will look and how many people will get it. Edit if needed.
5. Click "Send a test to me" and check the test in your own inbox. Tests are marked `[Test]` and go to nobody else.
6. Tick the confirmation box and click Send. Confirm when asked.

A broadcast can only be sent once, and cannot be edited afterwards. Each member gets their own copy, so nobody sees anyone else's address. The Broadcasts list shows drafts and everything sent, with how many were delivered and whether any failed.

## The inbox

Enquiries and audition applications from the Contact page arrive here, newest first, with unhandled items at the top. The number on the Inbox link is how many are unhandled. Each submission is also emailed to the choir's address.

- Mark Handled once someone has replied. Mark Not Handled undoes it.
- To clear spam, tick the items (or Select all) and choose Delete as Spam. This asks first and cannot be undone.

## Duty of care

Duty of Care lists every singer's emergency contact, medical and accessibility notes, and under-18 flag. Singers fill these in themselves under Settings. The roster is confidential: only the committee can open it. It works well on a phone, so it can be opened quickly at a rehearsal or concert.

## Documents

Documents is where minutes, policies and similar files go. The committee can upload and delete them, and every member can read them from the Document Vault.

## If something goes wrong

Every page that changes something shows a green banner when it worked and a red one when it did not, saying which field needs attention. If a page shows an error you cannot get past, take a screenshot and send it to me, with what you were trying to do. Django admin is still available to me as a fallback for anything the site cannot do.
